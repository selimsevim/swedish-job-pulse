How this works & its limits

The CV Job Fit Scanner runs in three layers:

  • Extraction — your PDF is parsed in your browser into skills, roles, languages and seniority. The file never leaves your device and is not stored.
  • Matching — your profile is scored against a role-skill index (skill overlap, role title, seniority and Swedish-language fit), enriched with public demand, crowding, regional and 4-week trend signals.
  • Gap analysis — what blocks stronger matches: missing skills, weak proof, language.

It reflects public Swedish job-ad data, not the entire hidden job market, and is not a guarantee of employment. The challenge build is evaluated on synthetic, fictional CVs only — no real or personal data is used or stored (data/cv_match_metrics.json).
