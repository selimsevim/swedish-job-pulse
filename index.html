How this works & its limits

Career Reality Check reads public job-ad signals from Arbetsförmedlingen / JobTech and combines them into one practical view:

  • Demand — how many active ads each occupation has now.
  • Trend — a 4-week demand forecast (a scikit-learn gradient-boosting model trained on weekly history, with a moving-average baseline). Where there is too little history, it falls back to transparent rules.
  • Crowding — search attention versus actual demand.
  • Entry-level & remote signal — how open a field is to beginners and to remote work.
  • Regional & skill signal — where a field is over-weighted, and which skills are growing.

It is not a guarantee of employment and does not replace professional career counselling. Use it as a labour-market reality check before deciding where to spend your application effort. Model evaluation lives in data/model_metrics.json.

Data sourced from Arbetsförmedlingen JobTech Development. Not a guarantee of employment.
